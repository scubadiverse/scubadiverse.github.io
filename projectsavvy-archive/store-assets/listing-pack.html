One thing to know first. Google makes brand-new personal developer accounts run a closed test with 12 people for 14 days before the app can go fully public. So plan for about two weeks of quiet testing before launch – that gate is Google's, not ours. Everything below is ready regardless.

The plan in 4 steps

  1. Create your Google Play developer account. Go to play.google.com/console, sign in with your Google account, pay the $25 one-time fee and complete the ID verification. Choose a Personal account. This part only you can do – it is tied to your identity.

  3. Fill the listing using the copy-paste boxes below, and upload the pictures + the app file I send you.
  4. Closed testing → add 12 testers → wait 14 days → then apply for Production. I'll help with testers.

Focus & Flow is a calm, private project management app that helps you work in kind, steady blocks - and reminds you to look after yourself while you do.

Set a focus block, pick a project, and start. When you have several things on the go, Focus & Flow suggests which project to give time to next, so nothing gets forgotten. It gently reminds you to drink water, rest your eyes, stand up and stretch - and after a long stretch online it gives you a real 2-minute time-out so you actually step away from the screen.

Feelings matter too. Tap how you feel and the app meets you where you are: box breathing, a simple grounding exercise, and calming rain or white-noise sounds when things feel like too much. Small wins are worth noticing, so you can log them and watch them add up.

Over time, Focus & Flow learns your own rhythm - your strongest hours and when you tend to tire - and shows your progress in clear charts: time per project, your pace, and a day-by-day trend.

- Focus blocks with a length you choose
- Up to 7 projects, each with a colour and a daily target
- A suggestion for which project to work on next
- Water, eye-rest, stand and break reminders
- A forced 2-minute time-out after too long online
- Mood check-ins with breathing, grounding and calming sounds
- A small-wins log and gentle encouragement
- Productivity charts and your learned focus rhythm

Private by design. Everything stays on your phone. No account, no sign-up, no ads, no trackers, and your data is never sold or shared.

Focus & Flow offers general wellbeing support and is not a medical device or a substitute for professional help.
