Privacy Policy – Project Savvy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

Project Savvy is a focus and wellbeing app. It helps you pace your work, take breaks, drink water, rest your eyes, calm down, and notice small wins. This page explains, in plain language, what information the app handles and how it is kept private.

The short version: the free app stores everything only on your phone, with no account. The premium app lets you sign in for cloud sync across your devices, and to optionally connect Google Calendar or Toggl Track. We never sell your data, and we never use ads or third-party trackers.

What the app stores

The app records only what you put into it and how you use it, so it can be useful to you:

  • Your projects and daily time targets
  • Focus sessions and the time you spend
  • Feelings / mood check-ins you tap, and any notes you add
  • Small wins, water logs, and your settings (reminders, block length, etc.)

In the free app, all of this stays only in the app's own storage on your phone.

Premium: account and cloud sync

If you sign in to Premium (email/password or Google sign-in), we additionally collect:

  • Your email address, to identify your account.
  • A device identifier, to enforce the plan's device limit.
  • The project/session data above, synced to our server (Google Firebase) so it's available on every device you sign in on.

This data is encrypted in transit and stored on Google Firebase infrastructure under our account. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Optional connections: Google Calendar and Toggl Track

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, finishing a focus block can create a calendar event (title, start/end time) via Google's Calendar API — only for sessions you add, and only after you grant calendar access. If you choose to connect Toggl Track, a finished session's duration and project name are sent to Toggl's API using the token you provide. Both are off by default and only activate if you connect them.

What the app does NOT collect

  • No health-device data. The app does not read your heart rate, stress, steps, or any data from a watch, Health Connect, Apple Health, or similar.
  • No location, contacts, photos, or microphone.
  • No advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers, no ads.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. It is shared only with the services above (Google Firebase for account/sync, and Google Calendar or Toggl Track if and when you connect them), solely to provide the features you asked for.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: open Settings → Profile → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account and all synced data (projects, sessions, settings) from our server. It does not affect events already created in your Google Calendar or Toggl Track, since those live in your own Google/Toggl account.

Free / local data: clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app permanently removes everything kept only on your phone.

By email: if you can't access the app, email contact@divescanner.net from the address on your account and we will delete it within a few days.

Children

This app is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. Please do not create an account if you are under the age required in your country.

Not medical advice

Project Savvy offers general wellbeing support (breathing, breaks, encouragement). It is not a medical device and is not a substitute for professional help. If you are struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or a qualified professional.
