Project Savvy

A focus and wellbeing app that helps you get things done without burning out.

What it does: Project Savvy breaks your work into focus blocks, reminds you to take breaks, drink water and rest your eyes, and helps you notice small wins - so you can concentrate and still look after yourself. It is designed to be gentle and ADHD-friendly.

Main features

  • Focus blocks with a simple timer and clear start/stop.
  • Wellbeing reminders: water, eye-rest, standing, and screen-time breaks.
  • Daily mood check-ins and small wins to track how you feel.
  • Projects and daily time targets to see where your time goes.
  • Premium: secure sign-in with cloud sync across your devices, and logging your finished focus blocks to your calendar (your phone's calendar or Google Calendar) as work-hours events.

Calendar & your data

The calendar feature only creates work-hours events when you ask it to or turn on auto-send. It does not read or delete your other events. You are always in control and can turn it off at any time. See how we handle your data in the Privacy Policy.

Get the app

Project Savvy is available on Android through Google Play. Open the app to start a focus block, or upgrade to Premium for cloud sync and calendar logging.
